**Fate Seeker** is a mystical astrology app rooted in the ancient Chinese divination system of Zi Wei Dou Shu (Purple Star Astrology) — a thousand-year-old tradition that maps your destiny through the positions of stars at the moment of your birth.

Simply enter your birth information, and Fate Seeker generates a comprehensive natal chart reading covering five dimensions of your life: your core personality and life path, career and wealth potential, love and marriage fortune, health and longevity, and your overall luck cycle across different life stages.

But Fate Seeker goes further than a one-time reading. Once your chart is cast, you can consult the stars on any question about your future — whether to change careers, when to start a business, or what lies ahead in your love life. The app draws on your unique natal chart to deliver focused, personalized guidance for whatever crossroads you face.

Available in both English and Traditional Chinese, Fate Seeker bridges millennia of Eastern wisdom with a modern, beautifully designed experience — so the stars are always just a tap away.
